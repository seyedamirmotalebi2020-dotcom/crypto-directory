Get in Touch

Questions, corrections, scam reports, or platform suggestions — we read every message.

Email

The fastest way to reach us is by email:

contact@land.me.uk

We aim to reply within 2–3 business days.

What to include

  • Report a scam or suspicious platform: the URL, what happened, and any evidence (screenshots or transaction records help)
  • Report a broken or dead link: the platform name and where you clicked from
  • Suggest a new platform: the URL, the category it belongs in, and why you think it's worth listing
  • Correct an error: which listing, what's wrong, and the correct information with a source if possible
  • Platform operator correction: if you run a listed platform and believe our information is inaccurate, email us — we correct verified errors
  • AdSense / partnership / press: tell us what you're looking for

What we can't do

  • We can't recover money lost to a scam. Contact Action Fraud (UK) or your local authority.
  • We can't mediate disputes with a platform — we're a directory, not a regulator.
  • We can't provide legal, tax, or financial advice.
  • We can't give a platform a guaranteed listing or score.

Response times

Most messages get a reply within 2–3 business days. Complex corrections, evidence reviews, or platform submissions may take longer. We read every message — even the ones we don't reply to individually.

We will never ask for your seed phrase or private key If anyone contacts you claiming to be us and asks for sensitive wallet information, card details, or bank credentials — it is not us. Report it and ignore it.
Reporting a scam is the most useful message you can send us Even a one-line email saying "site X asked me to pay a withdrawal fee" helps us warn the next visitor. It takes you 30 seconds and protects hundreds of people.
