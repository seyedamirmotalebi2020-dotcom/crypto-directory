Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

How much can I actually earn online?

It depends entirely on the category. Crypto faucets pay pennies per hour. Paid surveys pay £1–£10. CAPTCHA solving pays £0.50–£3. Freelancing pays £10–£100+ depending on your skills. We show realistic ranges on every category page.

Is this available in my country?

Most categories are truly worldwide. Some sites block specific countries due to regulation or payment restrictions — every listing shows its supported regions. Filter by "Worldwide" to see sites that accept users globally.

Do I need to pay to sign up anywhere?

No. Every site listed is free to join. If any site asks for an activation fee, verification payment, or "investment", it's a scam — we blacklist those and warn you on the site card.

What's the safest way to try a new site?

Sign up with a burner email, earn a small amount, and withdraw as soon as you hit the minimum. Only invest real time after a successful test withdrawal.

How are the trust scores calculated?

Each site is scored 0–100 from four signals: current payout evidence, site age and continuity, independent monitoring data, and community votes. Categories receive a trust score based on the median of their sites and the percentage still active.

Do you take payment for listings?

No. Some sites include our referral link in the Visit button, but this never affects the trust score or review. Scam sites are blacklisted regardless of whether they offer referrals.

Can I earn from multiple categories at once?

Yes, and that's the recommended strategy. Most successful users combine one higher-paying category (like surveys or freelancing) with one or two low-effort categories (like faucets or cashback) that fill spare moments.

Is earning online legal?

In most countries, yes. Earning from surveys, cashback, freelancing, and crypto is legal in virtually every jurisdiction. Tax treatment varies by country — check your local rules. Some gambling-adjacent sites are restricted in certain regions; we flag those.

What about taxes on my earnings?

In most countries, earnings from online work are taxable as ordinary income. Small amounts often fall below reporting thresholds, but the principle applies. Keep simple records — date, amount, source — and consult a professional if amounts matter.

Can I earn without giving personal information?

Most faucets and cashback sites require only an email. Some GPT platforms require ID verification before your first payout. Never provide a wallet seed phrase, private key, or bank login — if any site asks, it's a scam.
