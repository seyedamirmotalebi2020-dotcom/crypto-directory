Survey Sites That Pay PayPal

September 21, 2026 5 min read Paid Surveys

PayPal is the fastest, safest way to receive survey earnings in the UK. Here are the platforms that pay via PayPal, with their current minimum thresholds — ranked by trust score.

Why PayPal matters

PayPal takes the friction out of small payments. You can receive a few pounds without waiting weeks for a bank transfer, and you can spend it immediately with any retailer that accepts PayPal or send it to your bank in a day or two.

The alternative — gift cards — locks your earnings into a specific retailer. Bank transfers are slower but usually free. PayPal sits in the middle: fast, flexible, universal.

The platforms that pay PayPal

Prolific — £0 minimum

PayPal only, no minimum. If you earn £0.50 from a study, you can withdraw £0.50. This is the lowest threshold of any legitimate UK platform. Trust score: 82.

Toluna Influencers — £5 minimum

PayPal or gift cards. 251,000+ Trustpilot reviews at 4.2/5. Strong UK presence. Trust score: 76.

MOBROG — £5 minimum

PayPal or Skrill. Operates in 74 countries. High negative-review response rate. Trust score: 78.

Prime Opinion — $5 minimum

PayPal, Venmo, bank transfer, gift cards or virtual Visa. $5 minimum depending on region. Trust score: 68.

Qmee — low threshold

One of the lowest PayPal thresholds on the market. Fast withdrawals, but lower survey volume. Trust score: 65.

Ipsos i-Say — £5+ minimum

Cash or vouchers from around £5. Established global panel. Trust score: 78.

ySense — $10 minimum

PayPal and other reward options. GPT platform with surveys, offers and tasks. Trust score: 68.

InboxDollars / InboxPounds — $15 / £20 minimum

Higher thresholds but reliable payments. Trust score: 68.

PrizeRebel — $5 minimum

PayPal, gift cards and crypto. Trust score: 66.

Swagbucks — $5 minimum

PayPal, gift cards and crypto. Trust score: 72.

YouGov — £50 minimum

Highest threshold on the list. Points convert to cash or vouchers. Takes months to reach. Trust score: 68.

PayPal fees on survey payments

Receiving money on PayPal as a UK personal account is free for most payment types. If a panel sends money as a "goods and services" payment (rare), you may be charged a small percentage. Most legitimate panels send as "friends and family" or an equivalent that avoids fees.

You can withdraw PayPal balance to your UK bank account for free (standard 1–2 business days) or instant for a 1% fee.

What about gift cards instead?

Gift cards often pay a "bonus" — 5–10% more than the cash equivalent. For example, a panel might offer £10 PayPal or £10.50 Amazon. If you'd spend the money at that retailer anyway, the gift card is the better choice. If not, PayPal offers flexibility worth more than the 5% bonus.

What about crypto payments?

Some GPT platforms (Freecash, Swagbucks, PrizeRebel, ySense) also offer crypto withdrawals. Rates are usually lower than PayPal payouts because of network fees. If you want to hold crypto, this is a convenient route. If you want pounds, stick with PayPal.
